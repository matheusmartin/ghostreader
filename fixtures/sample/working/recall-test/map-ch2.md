## Chapter 1 [ch1]

[ch1.s1 · In short]
Ines, the keeper's daughter, finds the lighthouse clock stopped. Mr Hale, the harbour master, warns that her father will lose the tower, so she pays Ada, a clockmaker, with her mother's compass.
Why it matters: Under the keeper's rule, the lamp turns on the clock's hour, or the keeper loses the tower.

[ch1.s2 · scenes]
1. {On the first cold morning} Ines finds the clock in [the lighthouse] stopped, so the lamp does not turn; Tomas, her brother, says nothing. (with: Tomas, Ines)
2. At [the harbour], Mr Hale warns that the keeper will lose the tower unless the clock runs by Friday. (with: Ines, Mr Hale)
3. {That evening} Ada agrees on [the quay] to mend the clock, because Ines offers her mother's compass. (with: Tomas, Ines, Ada, Mr Hale)
4. {That night} Ines gives Ada the key at [the house], and the clockmaker climbs the tower. (with: Ines, Ada)

[ch1.s3 · Who they are]
- Ines: The keeper's daughter; she holds the key to the clock room while her father is away.
- Tomas: Ines's younger brother; he says nothing when she says somebody stopped the clock.
- Mr Hale: The harbour master; he says the clock is the family's problem, not his.
- Ada: A clockmaker from the city, come to repair the church clock.

[ch1.s6 · Where it stands]
- When and where: The night the clock stopped; Ines is at the house, and Ada is at the top of the tower.

- What they want: Ines wants the clock running by Friday, so her father keeps the tower.
- What just happened: Ines could not pay Ada's price, so she gave her mother's compass and the key.

## Chapter 2 [ch2]

[ch2.s1 · In short]
Ada finds salt packed into the clock's main wheel, so somebody used a key. Tomas admits he lent the key to Mr Hale; Ines will not give it to him again.
Why it matters: Only two keys open the clock room, and Mr Hale borrowed one.

[ch2.s2 · scenes]
1. {At dawn} Ada shows Ines salt from the main wheel in [the kitchen], so Tomas admits he lent Mr Hale the key. (with: Tomas, Ines, Ada)
2. {At noon} the mended clock strikes in [the lighthouse], so the lamp turns and the boats come in. (with: Ada)
3. {In the afternoon} Mr Hale thanks Ada and asks for the key, but Ines says her father will decide. (with: Ines, Ada, Mr Hale)
4. {When he has gone} Ines hides the key in the base of the lamp in [the kitchen]. (with: Ines)

[ch2.s3 · Who they are]
- Tomas: Ines's younger brother; he lent the key to Mr Hale for one evening, for a silver coin.
- Mr Hale: The harbour master; he borrowed the key and now asks Ines for it.
- Ada: The clockmaker; she found the salt, keeps it, and stays in Kessel to mend the church clock.

[ch2.s5 · Where it stands]

- Who is there: Ines, the keeper's daughter, alone, with the key hidden in the kitchen lamp.
- What they want: Ines wants her father to keep the tower; Mr Hale wants the key kept at the harbour office.
- What just happened: Mr Hale asked for the key, so Ines said her father would decide, and hid it.
